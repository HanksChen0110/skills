# Material Checklist

Use this checklist before creating or updating the personal showcase page.

## Required Inputs

- Personal positioning: one sentence that clearly says who CHL is and what role he targets.
- Current state and target role.
- Past identity tags: industry background, product experience, AI product experience, and any relevant tool or methodology capability.
- Target audience: recruiter, interviewer, colleague, or friend.
- Desired audience action: continue reading, contact, invite to interview, or understand the work method.
- At least one project story with: title, background/problem, CHL's role, key actions, result/output, and available proof material.
- Available assets: avatar path, screenshots, demo links, workflow diagrams, metrics, or feedback records if used.
- Visual and tone preference: professional, rigorous, lightweight, technology feel, reference screenshot, or layout preference.
- HTML output directory.

## Missing Material Rule

If any of these are missing, ask CHL to complete them before continuing:

- Personal positioning
- Target role or target audience
- At least one project story with role, actions, and result
- HTML output directory
- Contact method or explicit instruction to omit real contact details

Use the structure of `G:\vibecoding\我的AI作品包\01_项目资料.md` when asking for missing information. Ask only for the missing fields, not for the whole document again.

## Default Source Assumptions

- The project line is personal showcase, single-page static.
- The first version should be visible and editable, then improved through feedback.
- The page should help recruiters or interviewers quickly understand role fit, real project outcomes, and problem-solving process.
